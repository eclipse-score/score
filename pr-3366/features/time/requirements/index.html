Feature Requirement

Time Synchronization Data#

The following requirements provide the information that project-specific diagnostics (e.g. diagnostic jobs and trouble codes) and an independent validation of the Vehicle Time (e.g. Safe Car Time, integrated as a validation stage or running on top of the score::time API) need.

Time synchronization data
status: valid
security: NO
safety: ASIL_B
reqtype: Functional
valid_from: v2.0.0
version: 2
derived from: stkh_req__time__safe_vehicle_time, stkh_req__time__vehicle_time_status, stkh_req__time__vehicle_time_sync, stkh_req__time__vehicle_time_sync_data
satisfied by: feat__time

The score::time feature shall provide the following data for each received time synchronization update, with all local timestamps in nanoseconds:

  • sequenceId

  • sourcePortIdentity

  • preciseOriginTimestamp

  • correctionField

  • the local Sync ingress timestamp

  • the Vehicle Time predicted for the Sync ingress instant before the update

  • the applied peer delay and its validity
