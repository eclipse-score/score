“Collector” and “Individual checks” describe the configured required-check mechanism, not proof that every intended job is covered. “None” means that no validation checks are required, regardless of queue or up-to-date settings.

Two additional gaps are worth noting:

  • lifecycle’s required collector does not include its separate QNX jobs.

  • time has a QNX queue collector in its workflow, but that collector is not listed as a required check.
